# Security policy

Mowr handles people's files and their AI account connections, so we take security reports seriously.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not** open a public issue. Instead, either:

- use GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/mowfxy/mowr/security/advisories/new), or
- email **support@mowrai.com** with the subject "Security".

Include what you found, the steps to reproduce it, the Mowr version (Settings → About) and the impact you expect. We aim to reply within three working days and will keep you updated until it is fixed. We are happy to credit you in the release notes.

## Supported versions

Security fixes go into the latest preview release. Please update before reporting.

## Scope

In scope: the desktop app in this repository, the account Worker in `cloudflare/`, and the website at mowrai.com.
Out of scope: the AI providers themselves (ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Google Antigravity), Google Drive, and builds that other people made from this source.
